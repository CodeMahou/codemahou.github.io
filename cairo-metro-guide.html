Key Features & Capabilities

Built for daily commuters, students, and tourists exploring Cairo.

🗺️ Interactive 84-Station Offline Map

High-precision schematic map covering Line 1, Line 2, and Line 3 (including Rod El-Farag Axis & Cairo University branches) with Pinch-to-Zoom and station facilities.

🧭 Smart Route & Transfer Planner

Compares Shortest Route vs. Less-Crowded Transfer alternatives with station counts, travel time estimates, and Live Trip Tracker alerts.

🎫 Official 2026 Ticket Fare Calculator

Automatically calculates ticket color (Yellow, Green, Pink, Purple) and EGP prices for Public, Seniors (60+/70+), Military/Police, and Special Needs.

📍 On-Device GPS Nearest Station

Instantly locates the 3 closest Cairo Metro stations to your current GPS position with exact distance in kilometers—processed 100% locally on your device.

💳 Subscription Savings & Smart Wallet

Calculate monthly/quarterly pass savings, locate subscription offices, and simulate Contactless Smart Card Wallet balance deductions.

🌍 Landmarks Guide & 9 Languages

Explore Cairo's top tourist attractions with nearest metro stations, fully translated into Arabic, English, Russian, Chinese, Japanese, German, French, Spanish, and Italian.

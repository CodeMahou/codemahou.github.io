🚀 How to Connect & Use WiMeCo

Set up wireless control between your Android phone and Windows PC in less than 30 seconds.

1. Run WiMeCo on Windows

  1. Download WiMeCo-Windows.zip above and extract the folder on your PC.
  2. Launch WiMeCo.exe. A QR Code and a 4-digit Pairing PIN will appear on the main screen.
  3. If Windows Firewall prompts you, allow access on Private/Local networks so your phone can reach your PC.

2. Connect via Wi-Fi (Recommended)

  1. Connect your Android phone and Windows PC to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Open the WiMeCo Android app and tap the central Connect button.
  3. Choose Scan QR Code from PC Screen for instant 1-second pairing, or select Auto-Discover PC on Wi-Fi Network and enter the 4-digit PIN.

3. Or Connect via Bluetooth

  1. Turn on Bluetooth on both your Windows PC and Android phone, and pair them once in Bluetooth settings.
  2. In the WiMeCo Android app, tap Connect → Connect via Bluetooth and select your PC.
  3. Note: Over Bluetooth, WiMeCo automatically uses compact album art thumbnails so playback commands respond with zero lag.

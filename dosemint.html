DoseMint: Pill & Med Reminder

com.codemahou.dosemint

Never miss a dose again or struggle to manage complex medication schedules for your loved ones. DoseMint is the smart, all-in-one family medication reminder and treatment tracker with dynamic dose rescheduling, an offline catalog of 300+ medications, and 100% local-first privacy.

Get it on Google Play 🛡️ Privacy Policy

Key Features & Capabilities

Engineered for precision, family care, and complete data privacy.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Medication Profiles

Create dedicated profiles for yourself, parents, spouse, and children—complete with photos, blood type, drug allergies, and medical notes.

🔄 Smart Dynamic Rescheduling

Took your 8-hour antibiotic an hour late? Automatically recalculate and shift future doses based on actual intake time to maintain safe intervals.

🔍 300+ Offline Medications Catalog

Search over 300 popular medications in English or Arabic with instant Smart Auto-Fill for dosage form, strengths, and meal instructions.

⏰ Exact Alarms & Floating Window

Reliable background alarms with customizable ringtones and an optional floating confirmation overlay to confirm or snooze in one tap.

📦 Refill Stock & Doctor Follow-Ups

Track remaining pills with low-stock refill alerts and receive dual doctor appointment reminders 24 hours before and on checkup day.

☁️ Local-First + Google Drive Backup

All health records stay 100% private on your phone's local SQLite database, with optional encrypted backup & restore to your personal Google Drive.
